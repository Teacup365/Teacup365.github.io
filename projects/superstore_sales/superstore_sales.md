# Superstore Sales Analysis
📄[Case Study](/projects/superstore_sales/superstore_sales.md)

## Overview

Developed an interactive business intelligence dashboard to evaluate sales trends, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, and discount effectiveness. Utilized SQL for data analysis and Tableau for dashboard development, incorporating interactive filters and dashboard actions to enable dynamic exploration of business performance metrics.

## Business Question

- How is the business performing overall? 
- What drives profit? 
- Which products/customers are most valuable? 
- Where are losses occurring? 
- What trends exist over time? 

## Tools Used

- SQL / BigQuery
- Tableau
- Excel

## Process

1. Cleaned the data
2. Explored trends
3. Built visualizations
4. Created recommendations

## Key Insights

- Sales demonstrate consistent growth over time, indicating sustained customer demand
- Profitability varies significantly across regions
- Technology products generate the strongest sales performance and contribute substantially to overall revenue
- Higher discount levels are frequently associated with reduced profitability
- Customer purchasing behavior differs across segments

## Final Recommendation

- Review high-discount transactions to identify situations where margin erosion outweighs revenue gains
- Allocate additional sales, marketing, and operational resources to regions demonstrating strong profitability
- Leverage the success of top-performing categories by increasing inventory availability, marketing support, and cross-selling opportunities
- Investigate regions and product categories with lower profitability to identify operational inefficiencies
- Tailor customer engagement and retention initiatives based on purchasing behavior within each customer segment
